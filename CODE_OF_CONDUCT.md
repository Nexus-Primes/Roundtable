# Code of conduct

This is a place for people who like modding FromSoftware games and want it to be less painful. These are the rules, in plain words.

- **Be kind.** Disagree with an idea, not with a person. No insults, slurs, harassment or pile-ons.
- **Assume good intent, and ask.** Most "bugs" are a missing step. Say what you tried and what you saw.
- **Keep it on topic.** Roundtable, the mods it manages and the tools around it. Politics and drama go elsewhere.
- **Respect privacy.** Never post another person's save file, Nexus key, Discord webhook, messages or real name. If you post a log or report, check it for your own details first.
- **No cheating talk about online play.** Roundtable will not help get around the game's online rules, and help requests about doing so will be closed.
- **Credit people's work.** Mod authors and tool authors made what Roundtable manages. Link to their pages; do not repost their files.

## What happens when it goes wrong

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules. To report something, use the **Security** tab's private report for anything serious, or mention
the maintainer in the issue. Reports are read by one person and kept private.

This applies in every place the project is discussed: issues, pull requests and any chat space linked from here.
